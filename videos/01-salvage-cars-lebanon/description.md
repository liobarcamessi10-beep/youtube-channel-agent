## Description (English)

```
What to check before you buy a used car in Lebanon, especially an imported American one: how to tell if it's salvage or flood-damaged, from the VIN and Carfax report to the papers at the Nafaa.
✅ The full checklist is below. Screenshot it and take it to the dealer.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✅ 5 CHECKS BEFORE YOU PAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. The VIN (chassis number)
☐ Find it in 3 places: under the windshield, on the driver's door, and on the papers
☐ Do all three match?
☐ No peeled or replaced sticker?

2. The car's history in the US
☐ Run a Carfax or AutoCheck report on the VIN
☐ Check the title: Clean / Salvage / Rebuilt / Flood
☐ If Salvage: the price should be well below a Clean one
☐ If Flood: be very careful, electrical problems show up months later

3. Auction photos
☐ Enter the VIN on an auction archive site (Copart / IAAI)
☐ See where the damage was and how bad

4. Papers and the Nafaa
☐ Customs declaration: does the VIN match the car?
☐ Is the seller the owner, or do they have a legal power of attorney?
☐ No hold or lien on the car?
☐ Don't pay a deposit before this step

5. A mechanic you choose (not the seller's)
☐ OBD computer scan for stored faults
☐ Paint thickness on every panel
☐ Flood signs: rust under the seats, mud or sand under the carpet, damp smell
☐ Does the airbag light turn on and off at start-up?
☐ Test drive: uphill, downhill, hard braking, full steering lock
```
